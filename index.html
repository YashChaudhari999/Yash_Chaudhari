My 4 Participant Principles for Clearer Software

01

Observe Before Judging

Record exactly what happened and where friction arose before concluding whether a feature is good or bad.

02

Explain the "Why"

Always tie an action or hesitation to an unmet expectation, missing context, or misleading visual cue.

03

Separate Taste from Usability

Personal aesthetic preference is distinct from interaction blockers that impede other users.

04

Respect the Research Context

Follow testing guidelines, deadline commitments, confidentiality protocols, and researcher prompts.
